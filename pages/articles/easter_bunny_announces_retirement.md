Since 1572, the Easter Bunny has been leaving eggs scattered around gardens across the world. But this year, after his last season, he has announced his retirement. Spokesmen from BunnyCorp held a press conference yesterday, explaining that Mr. Bunny will be withdrawing from public view in order to eat carrots in his German cottage and spend time with his great-great-great grandchildren, the Passover Bunny and the July 4th Bunny. Much to the dismay of little children everywhere, egg hunts across the nation have been cancelled until a replacement can be found. Pope Leo XIV has formed a Holy Rabbit Committee, inviting people of the world to audition for the role. The jolliest applicant will reportedly be converted into a rabbit via ancient Christian techniques.
