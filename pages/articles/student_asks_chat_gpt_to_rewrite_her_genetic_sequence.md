Stuyvesant High School is known for having a stressful environment, with students using anything they can to better complete their studies. Caffeine-heavy drinks and sleepless nights are the norm among students, but one desperate junior recently modified her genetic sequence using OpenAI’s language model, ChatGPT. After failing her AP Cooking exam, Annita Brayk reportedly used the LLM to alter her own DNA in an attempt to grow her brain. When the chatbot received her request, it happily provided her with a set of instructions, including drawing blood, uploading her entire genetic sequence to Microsoft Word, and exporting it as a PDF. Then, the bot instructed Brayk to drink a mixture mainly containing nucleic acid (which she stole from the biology storage room). Brayk reportedly took the mixture on Wednesday night, going to sleep right after. She expected her brain to have doubled in size by the time she woke up. Instead, she contracted a form of severe meningitis, vomiting all over the library and soaking twelve editions of 目玉焼きにまつわる最高に素敵な物語, a popular manga series. After the Speculator’s writers asked ChatGPT for comment, it repeatedly denied the event, claiming that it was all a “cleverly edited viral video” and not an actual event.
