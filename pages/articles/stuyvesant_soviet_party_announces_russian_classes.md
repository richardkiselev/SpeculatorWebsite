Stuy’s World Language department has a wide selection of options for students to choose from. Most pick Spanish or Chinese, but French, Japanese, German, and Latin are offered as well. For SSP chairman Kingston G. Baranov, this was an injustice. In a press conference yesterday, he excoriated the “exclusion of the mighty Russian language” and proudly announced that in 2027, Russian will become an option for all students. This is part of the booklet containing all 793 of the SSP’s grand ideas, nicknamed “Project 2026” by fanfiction writers. The booklet advocates for the introduction of Russian classes, followed by the change of Stuyvesant’s official language in 2028. The SSP put out a notice to all publications, decreeing that newspapers and magazines must also be in Russian from now on. The Speculator will unfortunately comply, and will be changing its name to the Спекулянт, or “Spekulyant”.
