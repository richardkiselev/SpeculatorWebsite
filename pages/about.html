About

The Stuyvesant Speculator is a student-run publication which provided 100% factual news for all the student body. We are NOT satire and should be taken with complete seriousness in every event we cover. We provide reliable and truthful reporting on issues that Stuyvesant students care about ranging from intergalactic warfare to bug meal plans. If you want to hear more from us, subscribe to our newsletter for $99.00 a month or start writing for us by joining our discord!
